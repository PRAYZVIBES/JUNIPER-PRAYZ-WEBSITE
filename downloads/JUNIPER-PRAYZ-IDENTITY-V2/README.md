# JUNIPER & PRAYZ — Identity V2 kit

## Recommended master

Use **01 Conversation** as the primary identity. It appears in the website header and carries the central two-voice idea.

## Alternatives

- **02 Double Take** — compact monogram and embroidery
- **03 Open Room** — venue posters and intimate shows
- **04 Afterglow** — softer campaigns and limited pieces

## Folders

- `png` — transparent bitmap exports
- `svg-editable` — fully outlined vector artwork; no font dependency
- `fonts` — Instrument Serif, Instrument Sans and OFL licences
- `preview` — one-page comparison board

## Core colours

- Night `#151219`
- Aubergine `#24172E`
- Porcelain `#F7F1EA`
- Coral `#F16A5B`
- Periwinkle `#A9B6F4`
- Mist `#DAD5DC`

The marks are intentionally separate from the PRAYZVIBES solo identity. Do not recolour them in olive, wheat or gold, and do not combine them with field, road or journey motifs.
