# JUNIPER & PRAYZ brand voice

## Core position

**Bavarian roots. Open roads. Good noise.**

JUNIPER & PRAYZ speak as two real people, not as a lifestyle brand. The voice is grounded in Bavaria without using folklore, connected to nature without wellness clichés, and independent without sounding defensive. The music world feels lived-in: earth, weather, streets, gardens, food, friends, movement and rooms where people still look at each other.

## How the voice sounds

- Use **we** and **you**. Avoid detached phrases such as “the duo” or “the project” when the artists are speaking.
- Keep sentences short, physical and specific.
- Prefer earth, weather, road, room, field, food, people and sound over abstract “energy”, “journey” and “creative universe”.
- Independence is something we do: writing, releasing, travelling and connecting directly.
- Nature is part of daily life, not decoration.
- The good life is generous rather than luxurious: friends, music, food, movement, air and time.
- Confidence comes from clarity. Do not claim to be revolutionary; show the independent behaviour that can change how music reaches people.
- A little roughness is welcome. Corporate polish is not.

## Avoid

- generic “shared frequency” language without a concrete meaning
- design-studio explanations on the public artist page
- exaggerated claims about changing the industry
- luxury, fashion or spiritual-wellness vocabulary
- artificial Bavarian dialect
- sustainability claims that cannot be demonstrated
