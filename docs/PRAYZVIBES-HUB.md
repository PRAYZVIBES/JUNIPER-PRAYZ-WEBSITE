# PRAYZVIBES hub

The JUNIPER & PRAYZ homepage includes one consolidated PRAYZVIBES section with four direct partner routes.

| Route | Destination | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tip | `https://ko-fi.com/prayzvibes` | Voluntary one-time support; the visitor chooses the amount |
| Bandcamp | `https://prayzvibes.bandcamp.com/` | Digital music, releases and downloadable artwork |
| Merch | `https://prayzvibes-shop.fourthwall.com/` | Official PRAYZVIBES merchandise |
| CD and vinyl | `https://elasticstage.com/prayzvibes` | Physical music editions |

## Payment boundary

The website displays ordinary links and does not load partner-shop scripts or payment frames. Ko-fi and the selected stores handle checkout and payment. The JUNIPER & PRAYZ website receives no payment-card data.

## Content rule

Keep the section clearly labelled as PRAYZVIBES. It is a connected world inside the duo site, not a replacement for JUNIPER & PRAYZ content.
