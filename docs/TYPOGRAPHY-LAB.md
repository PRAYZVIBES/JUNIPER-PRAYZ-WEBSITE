# JUNIPER & PRAYZ typography lab

The private comparison page `font-lab.html` preserves three complete first-viewport directions using the same approved ingredients:

- the South Africa photograph
- the exact Wild Halo music-key symbol
- the current sticker colour palette
- the existing truthful website message and actions

## Option A — Two Voices Editorial

- Instrument Sans for identity and interface
- Instrument Serif for emotional statements
- Reference territory: cinematic duo photography, editorial restraint and minimal artist navigation
- Internal recommendation: strongest balance of Juniper warmth, PRAYZVIBES clarity and international artist-site polish

## Option B — Fashion Duo

- Bodoni Moda for the primary display identity
- Instrument Sans for navigation and interface
- Reference territory: fashion-led campaign art direction and high-contrast duo visibility
- Historical selection. The polished fashion-led result was rejected because it did not carry enough Juniper wildness or PRAYZVIBES weight.

## Option C — Organic Cinema

- Cormorant Garamond for the primary identity and intimate statements
- Instrument Sans for navigation and interface
- Reference territory: cinematic folk, natural materials and acoustic storytelling
- Internal assessment: most intimate and most connected to Juniper’s original sketch; less graphically forceful than option A

## Font files

All font files are stored locally in `fonts/`, accompanied by their SIL Open Font License files. They are loaded by the comparison page from the package itself; no external font service is contacted.

The `.ttf` files can also be installed on the project computer and used in Affinity Designer. Restart Affinity after installing new fonts.

The comparison page is marked `noindex,nofollow` and is intended only for local/private review. The production homepage now uses Wild Duality: condensed Instrument Sans structure, a restrained Instrument Serif gesture, documentary crops and a stronger opposition between organic Juniper and masculine PRAYZVIBES.
