# Brand audit

## Supplied legacy direction

The supplied PDF defines:

- `#5B93AE` blue
- `#CA9975` tan
- `#562935` burgundy
- `#A9CCCC` pale blue
- `#101222` navy

The exported logo and banner variants concentrate mainly on burgundy, tan, a broad all-caps sans serif and a repeating wave/heart pattern.

### Why this direction was not used

- It reads as a complete standalone lifestyle brand rather than a new musical world connected to PRAYZVIBES.
- The broad type has weight but very little tension or intimacy.
- The repeated pattern competes with photographs and performance video.
- Burgundy and tan dominate so strongly that the established PRAYZVIBES palette disappears.
- Juniper has explicitly rejected the colours and fonts.

The legacy banner and palette remain in `docs/reference/` for provenance only. They are not loaded by the website.

### J&P additions

| Role | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blush | `#D4A8B7` | Juniper’s additional warmth, ampersand, live strip |
| Deep blush | `#8B596C` | Accessible accent on light surfaces |
| Soft lilac | `#C7B7D6` | Reserved expansion colour |

Blush is deliberately an accent, not the foundation. This keeps J&P inside the PRAYZVIBES universe.

## Typography

No external web fonts are requested.

- **PRAYZ voice:** `"Arial Black", "Arial Narrow", Impact, sans-serif`
- **Juniper voice:** `"Iowan Old Style", Baskerville, "Times New Roman", serif`
- **Body:** `Inter, "Segoe UI", Arial, sans-serif` with system fallback

The heavy voice is used for structure and identity. The serif appears only where a softer, human counterpoint adds meaning — especially the ampersand, “meets” and the manifesto.

## Wordmark

The website uses a live typographic wordmark:

**JUNIPER** *&* **PRAYZ**

Advantages:

- remains sharp at every size
- loads instantly
- is accessible and selectable
- can evolve once Juniper approves the final identity
- avoids locking the site to a rejected bitmap logo

The ampersand acts as the visible meeting point between both worlds.

## Motion and form

Frequency is represented by restrained circular rings and orbit lines. The portrait frame uses an organic asymmetrical curve. These devices echo the idea of waves without reusing the old repeating pattern.
