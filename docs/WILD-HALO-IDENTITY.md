# WILD HALO — JUNIPER & PRAYZ identity

## The central metaphor

**One living line becomes sound.**

The mark begins as a juniper sprig: wild growth, intuition, resilience and Juniper’s preferred light, hand-drawn self-expression. The line moves into an interlocking central form that reads as an ampersand, then rises through three open halos: voice, frequency, shared resonance and the uplifting meaning behind **PRAYZ / praise**.

1. **Juniper Wild** — branch and human line
2. **Juniper & Prayz** — central meeting point
3. **Praise / PRAYZVIBES** — sound moving upward and outward

The antique-gold point marks where two energies become one frequency.

## Production character

The identity is related to PRAYZVIBES but remains independent:

- Frequency Black, Paper Cream and Antique Gold form the timeless bridge.
- Wild Sage carries Juniper’s organic calm.
- Real photography supplies emotion and credibility.
- Thin registration lines, measured grids and restrained circular forms create the Affinity Designer / editorial-print character.
- No generated illustration, character art or synthetic scene is used by the website.

The approved visual source of truth is the supplied Wild Halo direction board. Its oval seal, horizontal botanical/frequency line, rising gold halo, tall condensed lettering and controlled accent palette are translated into editable production elements rather than embedded as one flattened website image.

## Typography

The selected production families are included in the website package and can be installed for direct use in Affinity Designer:

- **Instrument Sans** — artist name, structural headlines, navigation, labels, actions, body copy and legal text
- **Instrument Serif Italic** — restrained organic gesture for Juniper's wild counter-voice

The website self-hosts the font files. No external webfont service is required.

## Signature

**WILD HEARTS / SHARED FREQUENCY**

This carries “Made of music and wild hearts” forward while expressing the duo relationship and the PRAYZVIBES connection.

## Minimum-use rules

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to four gold-point diameters.
- Use the full mark at 32 px or larger.
- Use the monochrome version for one-colour production.
- The website and 47 mm sticker system use the approved slight horizontal stretch of the exact music-key symbol.
- Never place it directly over a visually busy face or photograph.

The exact sticker symbol is now the public-facing master. The editable SVG studies remain in the package for future Affinity reconstruction, but they are no longer substituted for the approved symbol in the header, hero, identity chapter or footer.
