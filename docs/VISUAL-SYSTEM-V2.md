# JUNIPER & PRAYZ visual system v6 — Wild Duality

## Core idea

**Wild roots. Heavy pulse.**

Wild Duality is built from a productive collision. Juniper contributes instinct, thorns, organic movement and open air. PRAYZVIBES contributes darkness, weight, rhythm and uncompromising structure. The exact halo/music-key symbol is the point where both forces become one signal. Frequency Black and Antique Gold retain the PRAYZVIBES connection; Sage, Coral and controlled Violet registration create the independent JUNIPER & PRAYZ universe.

## Typography

The website deliberately uses reliable local font stacks so it remains fast, private and GitHub-ready without font services.

| Role | Stack | Character |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Artist name and dominant display | Instrument Sans, condensed and heavy | Massive, direct and rhythmic |
| Organic gesture and emotional counter-voice | Instrument Serif Italic | Human, imperfect and untamed |
| Navigation, labels and actions | Instrument Sans | Compact, direct and structured |
| Body and legal | Instrument Sans | Clear, human and consistent with the interface |

Rule: Instrument Sans carries the masculine weight and the shared artist identity. Instrument Serif Italic appears sparingly as Juniper's living gesture. Both fonts are stored locally and no external font service is contacted.

## Image treatment

All photographs use the same CSS treatment:

- 32% grayscale with a slightly lighter 27% hero treatment
- a restrained 13–14% sepia warmth
- controlled saturation rather than washed-out colour
- increased contrast and a darker 70–76% brightness curve
- full image opacity beneath a non-destructive overlay
- a light Frequency Black veil, soft sage/gold/coral registration and an edge vignette

This controls bright skies, gives the supplied photographs more depth and creates a tougher documentary family without hiding recognisable faces and places. The original files remain untouched.

## Interface rules

- Buttons and labels use hard rectangular geometry; selected Juniper elements break it with irregular organic radii.
- Frequency Black and Paper Cream establish the base. Sage embodies Juniper; Coral carries live energy; Gold connects both worlds.
- Instrument Sans carries scale and weight; Instrument Serif Italic is reserved for the wild human counter-voice.
- The exact Wild Halo music-key mark is enlarged, stretched slightly and given coral/violet registration shadows.
- Photography uses jagged poster crops, hard borders and offset print shadows rather than polished fashion frames.
- Decorative colour never replaces typographic hierarchy or real photography.

## Production imagery

The website uses only:

- the supplied South Africa photograph
- locally stored stills from the duo’s public Instagram reels
- the exact user-approved Wild Halo music-key artwork used by the 47 mm stickers
- a real-photo social card assembled from the supplied photograph

The visual character comes from crop, grayscale, warmth, colour overlays, typography and circular construction. Generated character scenes are not part of the production website.
