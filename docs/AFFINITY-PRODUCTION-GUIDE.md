# Affinity Designer production guide

## Wild Duality font system

The approved open-source TTF files are included in `fonts/`. Install them in Windows, then restart Affinity Designer:

| Role | Affinity font | Website CSS |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Artist display and structural headlines | Instrument Sans SemiBold / Bold, condensed | `"Instrument Sans"` |
| Organic Juniper gesture | Instrument Serif Italic | `"Instrument Serif"` |
| Identity, interface and body | Instrument Sans Regular / SemiBold | `"Instrument Sans"` |

Use Instrument Sans between 75% and 82% width for large statements, compact labels and navigation. Use Instrument Serif Italic only as a human counter-gesture, never as the primary wordmark. Convert final logo lettering to curves for external print handoff, but keep an editable text version in the Affinity source file.

## Layout construction

- Use a 12-column grid for landscape formats.
- Use 32 px or equivalent print-unit increments for secondary spacing.
- Keep strokes between 0.75 pt and 1.5 pt in print layouts.
- Use hard rectangles and jagged poster crops for PRAYZVIBES; reserve irregular organic radii for Juniper.
- Combine one massive condensed Instrument Sans statement with a small Instrument Serif Italic counter-gesture.
- Use real photography with stronger grayscale, contrast and sage/coral registration washes.
- Avoid artificial textures, generated characters, mock 3D effects and decorative clip art.

## Approved board translation

- The bold one-line JUNIPER & PRAYZ wordmark over the oversized music-key halo is the primary website gesture.
- The 47 mm circle is the supporting editorial/container shape.
- Botanical linework moves from left to centre; waveform linework moves from centre to right.
- Three open gold arcs above the ampersand represent the rising halo.
- Acid, coral and violet may appear as signal accents and controlled full-colour section fields.
- Never paste the full direction board into a page layout; reconstruct it with editable elements.

## Website-to-Affinity translation

- Header and navigation labels: Instrument Sans SemiBold, strongly condensed, uppercase and tracked.
- Hero artist name: Instrument Sans Bold Condensed; the ampersand may use Instrument Serif Italic.
- Emotional counter-lines: Instrument Serif Italic, used sparingly.
- Long copy: Instrument Sans Regular.
- Wild Halo chapter: Frequency Black grid with a hard-edged poster frame, print-registration shadows and the exact gold music-key artwork.
- Social card: 1200 × 630 px, real photograph, 55/45 image-to-type split.
