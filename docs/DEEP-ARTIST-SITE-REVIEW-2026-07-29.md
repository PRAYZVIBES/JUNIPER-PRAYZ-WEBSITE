# Deep artist-site review

Reviewed: **29 July 2026**

This pass compares the J&P website with ten established duo sites on the qualities that matter for a growing artist project: immediate identity, campaign focus, visible duo chemistry, media access, conversion, maintainability and technical restraint.

## Executive conclusion

The strongest reference sites are not “complete brochures”. They are active campaign surfaces:

1. one unmistakable world above the fold
2. one current reason to act
3. the duo or its mythology visible immediately
4. music, video or tickets closer than biography
5. an underlying system that can change with every era

The J&P design already had strong atmosphere, brand continuity and duo warmth. The deep review exposed one concrete visual defect — the hero word “FREQUENCY” could be clipped at a normal desktop width — and one strategic weakness: the first screen expressed a manifesto but did not expose a current playable moment.

Both issues were corrected:

- responsive hero typography was reduced and rebalanced
- the latest Annafest reel now appears as a playable hero action
- Instagram remains privacy-aware and loads only after deliberate interaction
- the site still avoids invented releases, dates or YouTube content

## Reference-by-reference findings

| Artist | Current homepage behaviour | What J&P should take | What J&P should avoid |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [First Aid Kit](https://www.firstaidkitband.com/) | Duo performance imagery shares the first viewport with an extremely direct ticket list. | Visible duo relationship and immediate live utility. | Letting dense listings overpower the emotional world. |
| [Angus & Julia Stone](https://www.angusandjuliastone.com/) | The official site currently offers less reliable public homepage access while commerce remains available separately. | Keep the core artist home independent and resilient. | Reducing the artist identity to a merchandise surface. |
| [Sylvan Esso](https://www.sylvanesso.com/) | A full campaign takeover for the current release, with expressive animated identity and pre-order focus. | Allow future J&P releases to reskin the first viewport. | Sacrificing legibility for experimental motion. |
| [ODESZA](https://odesza.com/) | Minimal navigation over a highly immersive visual field; the symbol carries the mythology. | Confidence, restraint and a small number of strong actions. | Depending on background video when the project does not yet own suitable footage. |
| [Twenty One Pilots](https://www.twentyonepilots.com/) | Current album, global watch event, videos, tickets and merchandise are layered into one era. | Build around the current campaign once a release exists. | Adding commercial layers before the underlying content is real. |
| [The Black Keys](https://theblackkeys.com/) | Release artwork dominates, with tour and store immediately available. | Let one strong campaign image define an era. | Turning the homepage into a shop before J&P has a catalogue. |
| [Milky Chance](https://milkychance.net/) | Deep music, video, live, discography, merchandise and sustainability archive. | Long-term expandable information architecture. | Shipping an oversized archive too early or leaving old dates prominent. |
| [SOFI TUKKER](https://www.sofitukker.com/) | Both people are highly visible beside a precise typographic identity; music follows immediately. | This is the closest duo-visibility benchmark for J&P. | A fashion-clean finish that would erase J&P’s earthier PRAYZVIBES roots. |
| [The Chainsmokers](https://thechainsmokers.com/) | The current EP owns the first viewport; listening is the reason for the site. | Give every future release one dominant campaign slot. | Hiding the human duo completely behind campaign artwork. |
| [Daft Punk](https://www.daftpunk.com/) | A durable mythology supports a large archive of shop, discography and video content. | Build recognisable recurring signals over time. | Attempting mythology before the audience has enough shared history. |

## Remaining limits that design cannot invent away

The website can reach the next level when the project supplies:

- one horizontal professional duo portrait
- one official J&P release with cover and listening link
- one official YouTube performance
- confirmed live dates
- a final approved two-sentence biography

Until then, the truthful “Field Notes” strategy is stronger than creating a fake release campaign.

## System for future eras

The current homepage should remain the base. For each real release:

1. replace the hero supporting action with the release
2. add the cover or campaign image
3. make “Listen” the primary button
4. keep the current reel archive below it
5. preserve Story, Worlds, Booking and legal pages

This mirrors the best reference sites: stable structure, changing era.
