The EN/DE switch stores the selected language in your browser under juniper-prayz-language. The value remains on your device and can be removed through your browser's site-data settings.

4. Instagram reels

The four preview images are local files. Instagram is contacted only after you choose a reel. Your IP address and browser information are then transmitted to Meta/Instagram and further processing may occur according to your account and settings.
