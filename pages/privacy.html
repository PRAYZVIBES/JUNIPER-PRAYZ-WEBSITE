2. Hosting and delivery

This website is prepared for hosting through GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub. When the site is requested, technical information such as IP address, date and time, requested file, referrer, browser and operating system may be processed so that the site can be delivered securely and reliably. GitHub states that visitor IP addresses for GitHub Pages are logged and stored for security purposes.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing a secure and stable website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Details are available in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

3. Language preference

If you use the EN/DE switch, the website stores the selected language in your browser under juniper-prayz-language. This value remains on your device and is used only to display your preferred language on later visits. You can remove it by clearing this website’s local storage in your browser.

4. Instagram reels

The reel previews are local image files. Merely opening this website does not contact Instagram for these cards. An Instagram frame is requested only after you deliberately select “Play reel”. Your IP address and technical browser data are then transmitted to Meta/Instagram, and additional processing may occur according to your Instagram account and settings.

The external media request is based on your deliberate choice and consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG). Closing the player stops the frame from remaining embedded on the page, but data already transmitted cannot be retrieved by this website. For details, see Instagram’s Privacy Policy.

5. External links

This website links to Instagram, YouTube, PRAYZVIBES, Ko-fi, Bandcamp, Fourthwall and ElasticStage. These services are not loaded merely because their links are displayed. If you follow a link, the respective provider processes data under its own privacy terms.

Tips, purchases and payments take place directly with the selected partner. This website does not collect or receive payment-card data.

6. Email contact

If you contact us by email, the information you provide is processed to reply and manage your enquiry. Depending on the subject, the legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) or our legitimate interest in responding to messages (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Messages are deleted when no longer needed unless legal retention duties apply.

7. Retention and your rights

Personal data is retained only for as long as required for the stated purpose, to meet legal obligations or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Subject to the applicable conditions, you have the right to access, correct, erase or restrict personal data, object to processing, receive portable data and withdraw consent for the future.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data-protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for private-sector organisations in Bavaria is:

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, service providers or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page. Analytics, advertising pixels and newsletter software are not active in this package; the policy and technical controls must be updated before any such service is enabled.
